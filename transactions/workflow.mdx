# Order lifecycle

An order's status changes as stock moves against it. On accounts with the new order screens, nothing advances an order except what actually happens to it: receipts, shipments and closes. Older accounts use steps instead. See [Older accounts: steps](#older-accounts-steps).

## Order statuses

**Where:** Transactions → **Status** column and filter. The order page shows the same status as badges.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved with **Save draft** and not applied yet. Doesn't affect inventory or planning. |
| **In Progress** | Applied and open. Its open quantity counts as on order. |
| **Received** | Everything ordered has arrived, but the order hasn't been closed yet. |
| **Completed** | Closed: received in full, or closed short, cancelled, written off or superseded. |
| **Archived** | Taken off the working list. Inventory is unchanged and you can restore it. |

On the order page the phase band shows **Ordered**, **Shipped**, **Received** and **Closed**, each marked **Nothing reported yet**, **Under way**, **Some reported, some still outstanding** or **Complete**.

## What does Received status mean vs Completed?

**Where:** Transactions → **Status** filter and column.

**Received** means every unit ordered has been received, but the order is still open. You can still receive extra units, and it hasn't been closed. **Completed** means the order has been closed, whether received in full or closed short, cancelled, written off or superseded. Closing a fully received order records it as received in full. An order that's partly received stays **In Progress**, and its lines show **Open** quantities and **% received**.

If your organization ties orders to shipments, orders can also read **In transit** or **Part in transit** once their shipment has left (Settings → **Shipments** → **Orders follow shipment status**).

## How do I receive part of a PO?

**Where:** Transactions → **Receiving**, or the order → **Report a receipt**.

Receive only what arrived. The line keeps the rest open and the order stays **In Progress** until everything arrives or you close it. Steps: [Receiving](/transactions/receiving#how-do-i-record-a-partial-delivery).

## What happens when I close an order?

**Where:** order page → **⋯** → **Close order…**. For one line: **Lines** tab → **Close**.

Closing stops Tether expecting the remaining units. They no longer count as on order, and planning stops counting on them. You say why:
- **Close short**: accept what arrived. Not counted against the supplier.
- **Cancel**: withdrawn or the supplier failed. Counted against the supplier.
- **Write off**: lost in transit. Writes the units off from stock.
- **Superseded**: re-ordered elsewhere.
- **It all arrived**: receives the rest, then closes.

The reason, who closed it and when are kept on the order. Closed lines read, for example, "Closed short — 40 not coming". To undo, use **Reopen order** (or **Reopen** on the line). Steps: [How do I close a PO short?](/transactions/detail-view#how-do-i-close-a-po-short).

## Older accounts: steps

**Where:** accounts without the new order screens. The order page's **Steps & Inventory** tab.

On older accounts, an order moves **Draft → In Progress → Completed → Archived** through steps:
- A purchase has a purchase step and shipment steps.
- A transfer has shipment steps.
- A production order has production steps.

You submit a draft to create its steps, then update each step as goods ship and arrive. The order completes when every step is done. To change a step's dates or quantities, open the order's **Steps & Inventory** tab.
