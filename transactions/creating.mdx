# Creating orders

You create purchase orders, transfers, production orders and sales orders from the **Transactions** page. Which button you see depends on your account:
- **New order** (with **Import orders** and **Report production**): most accounts. Opens the order editor described below.
- **Create Draft Transaction**: older accounts that haven't moved to the new order screens. See [Older accounts: Create Draft Transaction](#older-accounts-create-draft-transaction).

You need permission to create transactions. If you see neither button, ask your admin.

## How do I create a purchase order?

**Where:** Transactions → **New order** → **Kind** (or **Type**) = **Buy — purchase**.

1. Go to **Transactions** and click **New order**. The **Create order** page opens.
2. In **Kind**, choose **Buy — purchase**. If your org has its own order types, the field is called **Type**: pick a type such as "PO · Purchase".
3. Choose the **Supplier** and **Default destination** (the location that receives the goods).
4. Optionally fill **PO number** (leave it blank and Tether assigns one), **Incoterms**, **Order date** and **Expected arrival**.
5. Under **Items**, click **Add line** for each SKU. Enter **Ordered** quantity, **Unit**, **Unit price** and **Currency**. A line can have its own **Destination** and **Final destination** if it goes somewhere other than the default.
6. Add freight, duty or other charges under **Other costs** if you know them.
7. Click **Preview & apply**. Check the changes, then click **Apply · N changes**. Nothing is saved until you apply.

Or click **Save draft** to finish later.

## How do I create a transfer between warehouses?

**Where:** Transactions → **New order** → **Kind** = **Move — transfer**.

1. Click **New order** and choose **Move — transfer** (or your org's transfer type, such as "ITR · Transfer").
2. Choose the **Source** location (where stock leaves) and the **Default destination**.
3. Optionally fill **Reference**, **Order date** and **Expected arrival**.
4. Add lines with **Add line**: SKU, **Ordered** quantity, and **Lot** if you track lots.
5. Click **Preview & apply**, then **Apply**.

## How do I create a production order?

**Where:** Transactions → **New order** → **Kind** = **Make — production**.

1. Click **New order** and choose **Make — production**.
2. Choose the **Default destination** (where finished goods land), and optionally a **Reference**, **Order date** and **Expected arrival**.
3. Add a line for each finished SKU. Choose its BOM if it has more than one.
4. Click **Preview & apply**, then **Apply**.

To record a run that has already happened, use [Report production](/transactions/report-production) instead.

## How do I create a sales order?

**Where:** Transactions → **New order** → **Kind** = **Sell — sales order**, or Sales → Orders → **Create Manual Order**.

Choose **Sell — sales order**, then the channel, **Ship from**, recipient and lines. Details: [Sales orders](/sales/orders#how-do-i-create-a-manual-sales-order).

## Where are my draft orders?

**Where:** Transactions → **Drafts** tab, or **View drafts** on the Create order page.

**Save draft** keeps an order you haven't applied yet. Drafts don't affect inventory or planning.
- Find them on the **Drafts** tab of Transactions, or click **View drafts** in the order editor.
- The list shows **Kind**, **Reference** (or "Untitled draft"), **Lines**, **Created by** and **Last saved**.
- Click a draft to carry on editing. When it's ready, click **Preview & apply**.
- To discard one, use **Delete**.

## How do I import orders from a file, spreadsheet or CSV?

**Where:** Transactions → **Import orders**.

**Import orders** opens the **Import order** page, which takes one order as a JSON document (an `ImportOrder`: external reference, sequence and the order's desired contents). It is meant for orders exported from another system. It does not read spreadsheets or CSV files.
1. Click **Import orders**.
2. Upload the `.json` file or paste its contents.
3. Click **Preview & apply** to see exactly what will change, then **Apply**. Tether opens the new order.

**To load a batch of purchase orders from a spreadsheet or CSV**, attach the file in the in-app agent chat and ask, for example, "Import these POs from this file." The agent reads the file, shows you the orders it will create, and creates them once you confirm.

## How do I edit an order?

**Where:** Transactions → the order's **⋯** menu → **Edit order**, or **Edit order** on the order page.

Edit opens the same editor on the existing order. Change the header or lines, then click **Preview & apply**. The preview lists exactly what changes. If someone else changed the order in the meantime, you'll see a fresh preview to check again. You can change the **Type** to another type of the same kind. Small quantity changes can also be made right on the order page's **Lines** tab. See [The order page](/transactions/detail-view).

## How do I turn a recommendation into an order?

**Where:** Supply Chain → **Purchase**, **Transfer** or **Production**, then **Supply Plan**.

Recommendations open in the same order editor, so you can change quantities, dates and lines before you accept. Accepted recommendations become orders from the **Supply Plan**. See [Working with recommendations](/supply-planning/recommendations) and [Supply Plan](/supply-planning/execution-plan).

## Older accounts: Create Draft Transaction

**Where:** Transactions → **Create Draft Transaction** (accounts without the new order screens).

1. Click **Create Draft Transaction**.
2. Under **Transaction Type**, choose **Buy New Inventory** (purchase from a supplier) or **Move Inventory** (transfer between locations).
3. Choose the **Supplier** (for Buy) or **Source Location** (for Move), and the **Destination Location**.
4. Click **Create Draft Transaction**. Then open the draft to add products, and submit it when ready.

If your account shows **New order** instead, use the steps above.
