# The order page

Click an order on **Transactions** to open its page. Most accounts get the order page described here. Older accounts get the step-based page instead. See [Older accounts: the step-based page](#older-accounts-the-step-based-page).

## What's on the order page

**Where:** Transactions → click an order.

The header shows the order's reference and badges such as **Received**, **Closed**, **Archived** or **No destination**. An order with no destination isn't counted as on order and can't be received until you set one. A band shows how far the order has got: **Ordered**, **Shipped**, **Received**, **Closed**.

Header buttons:
- **Report a receipt**: record goods arriving. See [Receiving](/transactions/receiving).
- **Edit order**: open the full order editor.
- **⋯** (**More order actions**): **All orders**, **Reopen order**, **Close order…**, **Correct supplier…**, **Archive…** / **Restore**.

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Header fields: **Reference**, **Type**, **Status**, **Source**, **Destination**, **Ordered date**, **Expected arrival**, **Incoterms**, **Ship to**, **Internal notes**, **Instructions** and more. Also the **Case file** card. |
| **Lines** | Each line with **Ordered**, **Shipped**, **Received**, **Open** and **% received**, plus dates, lot and destination. |
| **Activity** | Everything recorded on the order, with a **Show** filter. |
| **Documents** | The order's paperwork (PO PDF and others) to view or export. |
| **Terms** | Purchase orders: **Payment terms**, **Receipt tolerance**, **Confirm by** and the due schedule. |

**Close order**, **Reopen order** and **Terms** aren't on every account. If you don't see them, ask Tether to turn them on.

## How do I edit quantities on an order?

**Where:** order page → **Lines** tab → click a cell. Or **Edit order** in the header.

1. On the **Lines** tab, click the cell you want to change (for example **Ordered** or **Expected arrival**) and type the new value. Changed cells are marked and can be reverted one by one.
2. A bar shows **N unsaved changes**. Nothing is written to the order until you save.
3. Click **Save changes**, or **Discard** to throw them away.

For bigger changes, such as adding lines, changing the supplier or splitting a line across warehouses, click **Edit order**. That opens the full editor with **Preview & apply**. See [Creating orders](/transactions/creating#how-do-i-edit-an-order).

On the Lines tab:
- Switch between **Line list** and **Size run** (if your PO template uses size runs). **Export** downloads the size run as **CSV** or **Excel (.xlsx)**.
- Use **Search lines** to find a SKU.
- **% received** can go above 100% when more arrived than was ordered.

## How do I download the PO PDF to send to my supplier?

**Where:** order page → **Documents** tab → **Export** → **PDF**.

1. Open the order and go to **Documents**.
2. On the document's row, click **View** to see it, or **Export** → **PDF** (or **CSV**).
3. If your organization has more than one template, pick one under **Template** first.

The tab says why a template was picked, for example because the order's type prints as that template. If a PO is split across shipments, the PDF prints the whole-order page first, then one page per shipment. To change what the PDF looks like, see [Document templates](/administration/document-templates).

## How do I close a PO short?

**Where:** order page → **⋯** → **Close order…**.

1. Click **⋯** → **Close order…**. The dialog says how many units are still expected.
2. Under **What happened to the rest?**, choose:

| Choice | Use it when | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **It all arrived: receive the N still expected, then close** | Everything came but wasn't recorded | Receives the rest today at each line's destination |
| **Close short** | You accept what arrived and stop waiting | Not counted against the supplier |
| **Cancel** | The order was withdrawn or the supplier failed | Counted against the supplier |
| **Write off** | Goods were lost in transit | Writes the units off from stock |
| **Superseded** | You re-ordered elsewhere | Not counted against the supplier |

3. Optionally add a **Note**, then click **Close order**.

The order page then shows the reason, who closed it and when. To close just one line, use the line's **Close** menu on the **Lines** tab. It has the same choices. If the order is on a shipment, closing short also takes the missing units off the shipment.

## How do I reopen a closed order?

**Where:** order page → **⋯** → **Reopen order**. For one line: **Lines** tab → **Reopen**.

Click **⋯** → **Reopen order**. The order goes back to open, and its remaining quantity counts as on order again. To reopen a single closed line, use **Reopen** on that line.

## How do I archive or restore an order?

**Where:** order page → **⋯** → **Archive…** / **Restore**, or the **⋯** menu on the Transactions list.

Archiving takes an order off the working list without changing inventory. You can restore it any time. If units are still expected, the button reads **Stop expecting them (cancel), then archive**. Use **Delete** (Transactions list row menu) only for orders nothing has been posted against.

## How do I correct the supplier on a PO?

**Where:** order page → **⋯** → **Correct supplier…**.

Pick the right supplier and give a **Reason**, then click **Correct supplier**. This also updates lots already received from the order, including the lot page, trace and recall report.

## How do I see the history of changes to an order?

**Where:** order page → **Activity** tab.

**Activity** lists everything recorded on the order: edits, receipts, closes and reopens, with who and when. Use **Show** to narrow it down.

## How do I attach or find documents on an order?

**Where:** order page → **Documents** tab.

**Documents** lists the paperwork attached to the order, with **Document**, **Reference** and **Created**. Rows marked **View only** can be viewed but not exported. Invoices show here for reference only.

There's no upload button on the tab. To attach a supplier invoice, packing list or receipt to an order, ask the in-app agent, for example: "Attach this invoice to PO 1234." The agent shows what it will attach and asks you to confirm.

## Older accounts: the step-based page

**Where:** Transactions → click an order (accounts without the new order screens).

Older accounts see tabs **Overview**, **Steps & Inventory**, **Documents**, **Inventory Impact** and **Analysis**. **Steps & Inventory** and **Analysis** only appear when the order has steps. Progress is recorded by updating steps. See [Order lifecycle](/transactions/workflow#older-accounts-steps).
