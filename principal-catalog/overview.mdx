# Products overview

**Products** is the sidebar section where you manage everything you make, buy and sell: your SKUs, the SKU groups that organize them, their bills of materials (BOMs), and the attributes (Size, Color…) that describe them. Earlier versions of these docs called it the "Principal Catalog".

## Where do I manage my products?

Where: sidebar → **Products**.

The **Products** section has four items, in this order:

| Item | Page | What it holds |
|------|------|---------------|
| **SKUs** | `/principal/skus` | Every item you make, buy or sell, typed as Finished Good, Variant, Bundle, Kit, Virtual Bundle or Component |
| **SKU Groups** | `/principal/sku-groups` | Primary SKU Groups (a product and its variants) and Standard groups (flat lists). These replaced Collections. |
| **BOMs** | `/principal/boms` | Bills of materials: which components make which SKUs |
| **Attributes** | `/principal/attributes` | Size, Color, Material and other attributes, each with an ordered list of values. Formerly called dimensions. Shown only if you have the `attribute:read` permission. |

The item names follow your organization's [terminology](/administration/terminology), so your sidebar may say **Styles** instead of **SKU Groups** or **Recipes** instead of **BOMs**. The section label **Products** itself is never renamed.

Every SKU belongs to exactly one **Primary SKU Group**, and can also belong to any number of Standard groups.

## Why don't I see a Catalog menu?

Where: sidebar → **Products** (new catalog) or **Catalog** (legacy catalog).

Your organization sees one of two sidebar sections, never both:

- **Products** — the current catalog. Every organization is moving to it. If you see **Products**, use these pages.
- **Catalog** (with **Collections** and **SKUs**) — the legacy catalog, shown only to organizations that haven't moved yet. See [Legacy catalog](/product-catalog/legacy-catalog).

Organizations on Products are sent to the new pages automatically: old links to `/skus`, `/collections` and `/collections/attributes` open **SKUs**, **SKU Groups** and **Attributes** under Products.

If you see neither section, your role doesn't have access to the catalog. Ask an administrator.

## Who can edit SKUs?

Access comes from your role's permissions, not from a fixed role name. See [Roles and permissions](/roles-permissions).

| Permission | What it lets you do |
|------------|---------------------|
| `catalog:access` | See the **Products** section in the sidebar |
| `catalog:read` | Read SKUs, SKU groups and BOMs |
| `catalog:write` | Create, edit, archive and delete SKUs, SKU groups and BOMs; edit the **Purchasing**, **Production**, **Storage** and **Sales** tabs |
| `attribute:read` | See **Attributes** in the sidebar |
| `network:write` | Edit policies, a SKU's **Default cost** and its **Vendor codes** |

If you can open a page but don't see its edit controls (pencils, **Create SKU**, **Import CSV**), you have read access only. Ask an administrator for the missing permission.
