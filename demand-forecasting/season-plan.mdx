# Season Plan and Hybrid Demand Plan (previews)

Two screens under **Demand** are previews turned on for individual organizations. If you don't see them, they aren't turned on for you; ask Tether if you're interested.

## What is the Season Plan page for?

Where: **Demand** → **Season Plan** (`/demand-forecast/season-plan`), for organizations with the `demand-targets` setting.

The Season Plan is a top-down seasonal plan:

- Set a season dollar target, cascade it down your product hierarchy, spread it across months, and convert it to units through your prices.
- It has two layouts: **MFP** (the year's two halves side by side against history, target against current, with carryover and new lines) and **Season** (one half's cascade with units, curves and mix).
- It reads your real sales, prices and hierarchy. It also reads any windows on the [Price Calendar](/marketing-promotions/price-calendar) for each month's average unit retail.

<Warning>
  The Season Plan is a draft: edits stay in your browser tab and nothing is saved. Use **Reset all edits** to start over.
</Warning>

## How do I set a seasonal curve for a new season?

Where: **Demand** → **Season Plan** → pick the season (Fall or Spring and the year).

The Season Plan spreads a target across months by curves measured from your own sales history; you plan the season by setting the target and adjusting rows, not by drawing a curve. Because the page doesn't save, it can't yet set the curve your forecast uses. To shape a new SKU's forecast from a seasonal pattern today, ask the agent (it can seed a forecast from a seasonal profile), or edit the forecast on the [Demand Dashboard](/demand-forecasting/dashboard).

## What is the Hybrid Demand Plan?

Where: **Demand** → **Hybrid Demand Plan** (`/demand-forecast/hybrid-plan`), for organizations with the `demand-hybrid-preview` setting.

A preview of a rep-level demand planning screen built on one customer's account and license structure. A permanent banner says the quantities are invented: the structure is real but the numbers are mock data, and edits are not saved. Don't use it to plan.
