# Filtering & Grouping

This page covers grouping and filtering on the standard [Demand Dashboard](/demand-forecasting/dashboard). If your organization uses the [demand plan](/demand-forecasting/demand-plan), group with **Rows** or **Group by** and filter with **Filter** in its settings bar instead.

Labels follow your organization's terms: "SKU group" may read **Collection**, "channel" may read your own word, and hierarchy levels show the names your organization gave them.

## How do I group the forecast by channel then SKU?

Where: **Demand** → **Dashboard** → **Grouping** → **Configure Grouping**.

1. Click **Grouping** in the toolbar.
2. In **Configure Grouping**, add levels from **Available Options** to **Selected Grouping Order**: for example the channel, then the SKU.
3. Drag the selected levels into the order you want. The first level is the outermost group.
4. Click **Save Grouping**.

Available levels:

| Level | What it groups by |
|-------|-------------------|
| Channel | Each sales channel |
| Channel hierarchy levels | Your channel hierarchy fields (for example Region, Account) |
| SKU group (Collection) | The SKU's primary group |
| Product hierarchy levels | Your product hierarchy fields (for example Category, Line) |
| SKU | Each SKU |
| Attributes | Each attribute your organization defines |

You must keep at least one level ("Please select at least one grouping level"). Expand or collapse a group with its row arrow.

## How do I filter the forecast to one product group?

Where: **Demand** → **Dashboard** → the filter chips in the toolbar.

1. Click the filter for the SKU group (your organization's word for it, for example **Collection**).
2. Pick the group or groups to keep.
3. Add more chips the same way: channel, SKU, a channel or product hierarchy level, or an attribute.

Filters combine: a row must match every chip. Remove a chip to clear it. **Table Settings** → **Show all filters** keeps every filter chip visible in the bar.

## How do I change the dates or periods?

Where: **Demand** → **Dashboard** → **Date Range** chip, and **Table Settings** → **View Type**.

- **Date Range** sets the first and last dates shown.
- **View Type** sets the columns: **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Custom Periods** if your organization uses its own fiscal periods.

## Can I save my grouping?

Where: **Demand** → **Dashboard** → **Saved views**.

Yes. Grouping, filters and display settings save together as a view.

1. Set the grouping and filters you want.
2. Open **Saved views**, type a name in **View name...**, and click **Save current view**.
3. To change a loaded view, adjust it and click **Update "…"**; or **Save as new view** to keep both.
4. Click **Set as default** beside a view to open the page with it. Views marked "Set up for you by an admin" were shared by an admin.

## How do I hide empty rows?

Where: **Demand** → **Dashboard** → **Table Settings** → **Hide 0s**.

Tick **Hide 0s** to hide zero values so the rows with a forecast stand out.
