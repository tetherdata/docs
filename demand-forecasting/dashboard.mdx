# Demand Dashboard

The Demand Dashboard shows the forecast for every SKU and channel by period, and is where you edit it. (Older docs called this page "Demand Forecast" or the "Forecast Dashboard".)

## Where do I see the demand forecast?

Where: **Demand** → **Dashboard** in the sidebar (`/demand-forecast`).

- The page title is **Demand Forecast**. Rows are SKU × channel pairings, grouped by the grouping you choose; columns are periods.
- You need demand read access to see the **Demand** group. Editing needs forecast write access.

<Info>
  **Why does my Dashboard look different from these docs?** Some organizations have the multi-hierarchy demand plan turned on. For them, **Demand** → **Dashboard** opens the demand plan instead of the page described here: a settings bar with **Rows**, **Filter**, **Time**, **Measures** and **Versions** (or your plan's own controls such as **Group by** and **Save Edits To**), and saved versions shown under **Live**. See [Demand plan](/demand-forecasting/demand-plan). If you'd like it, ask Tether to turn it on.
</Info>

## How do I switch the forecast to weekly or monthly?

Where: **Demand** → **Dashboard** → **Table Settings** → **View Type**.

1. Click **Table Settings** in the toolbar.
2. Under **View Type**, pick **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**. **Custom Periods** appears when your organization uses its own fiscal periods.

The same menu has **Density**, **Show all filters**, **Hide 0s**, **Show SKU name**, and an export option to download the table.

## How do I change the dates shown?

Where: **Demand** → **Dashboard** → the **Date Range** chip at the left of the toolbar.

Click **Date Range**, set the range, and apply. Remove the chip to go back to the default range.

## How do I show promotions on the forecast?

Where: **Demand** → **Dashboard** → **Table Settings** → **Show promo overlay**.

**Show promo overlay** is on by default; it marks the forecast cells your [promotions](/marketing-promotions/promotions) lift. Untick it to hide the marks. Promotions are created under **Marketing & Promotions** → **Promotions**.

## How do I see which model produced a row?

Where: **Demand** → **Dashboard** → **Table Settings** → **Show source model**.

**Show source model** appears for organizations with per-pairing model assignment turned on. It adds a chip on each row naming the model behind that pairing's forecast. See [Model assignment](/demand-forecasting/model-assignment).

## How do I group and filter the forecast?

Where: **Demand** → **Dashboard** → **Grouping** button and the filter chips in the toolbar.

- **Grouping** opens **Configure Grouping**: move levels (channel, channel hierarchy levels, SKU group, product hierarchy levels, SKU, and your attributes) from **Available Options** into **Selected Grouping Order**, then click **Save Grouping**.
- Filter chips narrow the rows by channel, SKU group, SKU, hierarchy level or attribute.
- Save the layout as a view from **Saved views**.

Details: [Filtering & grouping](/demand-forecasting/filtering).

## What is the "stale overrides" badge?

Where: **Demand** → **Dashboard** → the **… stale overrides** badge in the toolbar (shown only when there are some).

The badge counts manual forecast edits whose model output has since moved a lot. Click it to open the stale-overrides worklist. See [Model assignment](/demand-forecasting/model-assignment#stale-override-exceptions).

## How do I edit the forecast?

Where: **Demand** → **Dashboard** → **Edit Mode**.

Click **Edit Mode**, type new numbers (or switch the **#** / **%** toggle to adjust by percent), then **Save Changes**. **Exit Edit Mode** asks whether to save or discard. Full rules: [Editing forecasts](/demand-forecasting/editing).
