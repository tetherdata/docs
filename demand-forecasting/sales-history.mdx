# Sales History

Sales History shows what actually sold: totals by SKU, channel and period on the **Summary** tab, and the individual sales orders behind them on the **Orders** tab.

## Where is Sales History?

Where: **Sales** → **Sales History** in the sidebar (`/sales-history`).

Sales History used to sit under **Demand Forecast**. It is now in the **Sales** group, next to **Orders**. You need sales history read access to see it.

## Where can I see last month's sales by channel?

Where: **Sales** → **Sales History** → **Summary** tab.

1. Set the **Date Range** filter to last month.
2. Open the view type menu (**View Type**) and pick **Monthly** (or **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Custom Periods** if your organization uses its own fiscal periods).
3. Click **Grouping** and put the channel first in **Selected Grouping Order**, then **Save Grouping**.
4. Use the units / revenue toggle (box icon for quantity, dollar icon for revenue) to switch between units sold and revenue.

Filters available: **Date Range**, channel, channel hierarchy levels, SKU group, product hierarchy levels, **SKU**, attributes, and **Fulfillment Status**. Turn on hide-zeros once a filter is set to drop empty rows.

## How do I find a specific sales order in sales history?

Where: **Sales** → **Sales History** → **Orders** tab.

1. Open the **Orders** tab and set a filter (the tab asks you to "select filters above to view sales orders" until you do).
2. Narrow by **Date Range**, channel, SKU or **Fulfillment Status**.
3. Click the **Order #** to open the order.

Columns on the Orders tab:

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Order #** | The order number; links to the order |
| **Order Date** | When the order was placed |
| Channel | The sales channel |
| Location | The location that fulfills it |
| **Fulfillment Status** | Where the order is in fulfillment |
| **Ship Date** | When it shipped |
| **Fulfillment Locations** | Every location that shipped part of it |
| **External ID** | The order's ID in the source system |
| **Created**, **Submitted**, **Updated** | Record timestamps |
| **Manual** | **Yes** if the order was entered by hand rather than synced |

## Can I export sales history?

Where: **Sales** → **Sales History** → export button in the toolbar (either tab).

Yes. The export on the **Summary** tab downloads the table as shown (grouping, period columns, units or revenue). The export on the **Orders** tab downloads every order that matches the current filters, not just the page on screen.

## How do I compare sales to the forecast?

Where: **Demand** → **Comparison** → **Dataset** → **Actuals (Historical Sales)** and **Prior Year Sales**.

Sales History shows actuals only. To see actuals next to a forecast, or this year against last year, add **Actuals** and **Prior Year** as datasets on [Forecast Comparison](/demand-forecasting/comparison).
