# Transfers

The Transfers page plans stock movements between your locations: which SKUs to move, from where, to where, and when. It shows in-flight transfers, your committed transfer plan, and the solver's suggested moves in one place.

Where: Supply Chain → **Transfer**. The page header shows the plan freshness indicator ("Plan as of…").

## Tabs

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Schedule** | The transfer workbench — a grid of moves across locations over the planning horizon |
| **List** | Every transfer row, **Detailed** or **By load** (truckloads) |

The active tab is URL-driven, so deep links land on the right view.

Organizations that require approval for transfers also see a **Review staged (N)** button. It opens the [Supply Plan](/supply-planning/execution-plan), where accepted transfers are approved.

## Where is the transfers worksheet?

Where: Supply Chain → **Transfer** has only **Schedule** and **List**.

Transfers has no Worksheet tab; it was retired. For a SKU's week-by-week flow at a location, use the Worksheet on [Purchases](/supply-planning/purchases) or [Production](/supply-planning/production), or the network grid on [Connected Planning](/supply-planning/connected-planning).

## Shared controls

The Schedule and List tabs share one header:

- **Filter bar** — filter by Location group (pinned), SKU, primary SKU group, SKU group, and Location. Group labels follow your organization's [terminology](/administration/terminology).
- **Date range picker** — from/to dates with quick presets, defaulting to the next 90 days (matching the Schedule's horizon).
- **Saved views** — save the current filters, grouping, sort, and granularity as a named view and reload it in one click.
- **Table settings** — grouping, sort, display mode (Qty/$), the show/hide toggles below, the **Scenario** choice, and **Export CSV**.

### Show/hide toggles

Where: Supply Chain → **Transfer** → **Table settings**. **Show supplier receipts** is off by default, so supplier receipts don't show on the transfers list until you turn it on.

The **Table settings** popover carries four dataset toggles:

| Toggle | Default | What it controls |
|--------|---------|------------------|
| **Show in progress** | On | Open POs and in-flight transfers |
| **Show recommendations** | On | Uncommitted solver suggestions |
| **Show supplier receipts** | Off | Goods arriving from suppliers — inbound receipts, not moves you ship |
| **Hide 0s** | Off | Drop SKUs with no activity in range |

<Info>
  The solver plans a purchase as a move out of the supplier node, so inbound receipts ride the same feed as real transfers. They are hidden by default so the page shows only what you ship. Turn **Show supplier receipts** on to see them; receipt rows carry a muted **receipt** badge.
</Info>

## Build truckloads from transfer recommendations

Where: Supply Chain → **Transfer** → **List** → **By load**.

**By load** groups transfer lines into loads: the lines going down the same lane in the same window. **Build over** (7d, 14d, 30d, 60d, 90d) sets how many days of lines one load gathers.

Each load header has:

- **Accept load (N)** — accept every recommended line on the load.
- **Reject load (N)** — reject them, with a reason ([what each reason does](/supply-planning/recommendations#reject-a-recommendation-with-a-reason)).
- **Make trucks** — build trucks (or containers) from the load's accepted lines.

Next week's loads sit under this week's as **Next week · forward look**, collapsed. You can accept them early.

### Make trucks

1. Accept the lines you want to ship.
2. Click **Make trucks** on the load. Tether fills trucks up to the lane's truck capacity and pallet size.
3. Move a line to another truck, or change the quantity on each truck.
4. Confirm. The toast reads "Built N trucks".

**Make trucks** builds the trucks only. Each truck goes out as one transfer order when you approve it on the [Supply Plan](/supply-planning/execution-plan), which sends it to your ERP if one is connected.

If **Make trucks** is unavailable, hover it for the reason: **Shipments off**, no truck capacity for this lane, no pallet size, **Needs an accepted line**, or **No permission**.

### Can I split a transfer line across two trucks?

Where: Supply Chain → **Transfer** → **List** → **By load** → **Make trucks** → the line's split button.

Yes. In **Make trucks**, click the split button on a line ("Split this line, so part of it can ride another truck") and put the rest on another truck. Split lines are marked **(split)**.

## Drawers

### Node drawer

Clicking a (SKU, node) cell opens the node transfers drawer: all incoming lanes (other locations → this node) and outgoing lanes (this node → other locations) for that SKU, with **Incoming**, **Outgoing**, and **Net** totals in the header. Selecting a lane drills into that recommendation's full body — SKUs, coverage, need, and incoming supply — inline in the same drawer, with a back control to return to the lane list.

### Lane drawer

Clicking an origin → destination lane opens the lane drawer: the per-SKU transfer line items on that lane. A single-period lane opens straight into its recommendation body; a multi-period lane lists the periods first, each drilling into that period's recommendation.

From either drawer you can **Accept** a line or **Reject** it with a reason. Rejected lines are listed under **List** → **Dismissed** until they ask again, with **Restore**. See [Recommendations](/supply-planning/recommendations).

### In-transit drawer

Clicking a node's in-transit figure opens the in-transit drawer: the inbound transfer transactions landing at that node within the coverage window, with quantities and arrival dates.

<Note>
  The in-transit drawer lists transfer transactions only. Inbound POs attributed to the destination aren't itemized here, so the listed transfers may total less than the node's overall in-transit metric.
</Note>

## Editing and ordering

Accepted transfers go to the [Supply Plan](/supply-planning/execution-plan), where they are approved (**Approve transfer (N lines)**), placed, and sent to your ERP.

## Exporting

**Export CSV** in Table settings downloads the active tab's rows — exactly what the current filters, scenario, and toggles show. Under **By load**, the export lists every SKU line with its load.

## Best practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Start from the List for triage">
    The List defaults to showing everything — in progress, committed, and recommended — sorted chronologically. Sort by urgency for a triage pass, then open rows to act.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use the node drawer to balance a location">
    The Incoming/Outgoing/Net header tells you at a glance whether a location is a net receiver or net shipper for a SKU. Large outgoing totals alongside a low coverage position are worth a second look before accepting.
  </Accordion>


</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| No recommendations in the List | The scenario or toggles exclude them | In **Table settings**, set **Scenario** to **Tether's plan** and turn **Show recommendations** on |
| I don't see supplier receipts | **Show supplier receipts** is off by default | Turn it on in **Table settings** |
| In-transit drawer shows less than the in-transit metric | The drawer lists transfer transactions only; the metric also counts destination-attributed POs | Check open POs on [Transactions](/transactions/overview) for the remainder |
| A rejected transfer can't be found | Rejected lines move to the Dismissed view | Flip the **Queue / Dismissed** switch in the List (Detailed) and use **Restore** |
| There's no Worksheet tab | The Transfers worksheet was retired | Use the Purchases or Production Worksheet, or [Connected Planning](/supply-planning/connected-planning) |
