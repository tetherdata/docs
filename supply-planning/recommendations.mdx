# Recommendations

A recommendation is an order Tether suggests: a purchase, a production run or a transfer. You accept the ones you agree with, change the quantity first if needed, or reject them with a reason. The reason decides whether you see the recommendation again, and when.

Recommendations come from Tether's plan (each solve) or, for some organizations, from a recommendation engine's run. See [Where recommendations come from](/supply-planning/overview#where-recommendations-come-from).

<Note>
  Older versions of Tether had **Dismiss**, which hid a recommendation for 14 days. Dismiss is gone. You now **reject** a recommendation and pick a reason.
</Note>

## Accept a recommendation

Where: Supply Chain → **Purchase**, **Production** or **Transfer** → **List** (or the Schedule / Worksheet cell) → click the recommendation.

The recommendation opens in its details, with what it covers, why it was suggested and its rules.

1. Change the units if the suggestion is close but not right.
2. Click **Accept**.

An accepted order joins your plan. If your organization requires approval for that order type, it waits under **Pending review** until someone approves it on the [Supply Plan](/supply-planning/execution-plan). Without approval, it is approved straight away.

## Reject a recommendation with a reason

Where: any recommendation's details → **Reject**. On the Supply Plan, an order awaiting review also has **Reject** in its row.

The **Reject** dialog asks "Why? The reason decides what happens next." Pick one reason, add an optional **Note**, and confirm.

| Reason | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **Not this time** | Stays on the list as rejected, with your name and note. Tether stops suggesting it until the **Ask again on** date, including the same SKU at the same site from any supplier (or sending site, or line) and any week. |
| **Done another way** | The need was met some other way. Same as **Not this time**: rejected, hidden until the **Ask again on** date. |
| **Wrong number** | Stays on the list, marked under review, so the quantity can be looked at. Nothing is hidden. |
| **Wrong cost** | Stays on the list, marked under review, so the cost can be looked at. Nothing is hidden. |
| **Wrong date** | Stays on the list, marked under review, so the date can be looked at. Nothing is hidden. |
| **Never** | Hidden from now on, wherever it comes from, until someone with approval rights lifts it from the [Never list](#stop-tether-from-recommending-something-ever-again). |

After you confirm, a toast says what happened: **Rejected** ("Hidden until Nov 23."), **Rejected for good** ("Listed on the Never list.") or **Flagged** ("Marked under review; it stays on the list."). Every toast but **Never**'s has **Undo**. A **Never** comes back only through the Never list.

## How long until a rejected recommendation comes back?

Where: the **Reject** dialog → **Ask again on**; the default is in Settings → **Supply planning** → **Plan approval** → **Rejected recommendations**.

**Not this time** and **Done another way** hide a recommendation until the **Ask again on** date. The date opens on your organization's default, **Ask again after (days)**, which is 7 days unless an admin changed it. You can pick any later day when you reject.

On that day the recommendation can come back if the plan still needs it. It can also come back early if the shortfall got worse since you rejected it. It is then marked "was rejected 11/12; shortfall worse since", so you know it is not new.

**Wrong number**, **Wrong cost** and **Wrong date** never hide anything, so there is nothing to come back.

## Get back a recommendation you rejected by mistake

Where: the toast's **Undo** right after rejecting; later, Supply Chain → **Purchase** → **List** → **Rejected**, or **Production** / **Transfer** → **List** → **Dismissed**.

- Right after rejecting, click **Undo** on the toast (not offered for **Never**).
- On Purchases, open the **List** tab and switch to **Rejected**. It shows who rejected each buy, when, why, and when it asks again. **Restore** puts it back under **Suggested**.
- On Production and Transfers, the **List** tab has a **Queue / Dismissed** switch. **Dismissed** lists recommendations you rejected until they ask again, each with **Restore**. If it is empty, it reads "Nothing dismissed. Recommendations you reject appear here until they ask again."
- A **Never** is not here. Lift it from the Never list (below).

## Stop Tether from recommending something ever again

Where: reject with **Never**; to undo, Settings → **Supply planning** → **Never list**.

1. Open the recommendation and click **Reject**.
2. Choose **Never**.
3. Under **Never, for:** choose where it holds:
   - Purchases: **From this supplier** or **Into this receiving site**.
   - Transfers: **Out of the sending site** or **Into the receiving site**.
   - Production: **At this co-man**.
4. Confirm. The toast reads "Rejected for good".

The **Never list** shows every recommendation rejected for good, with its kind, where it holds, the note, who set it and when, plus a **History** of sets and lifts. Its **Lift** button needs approval rights. Anyone else sees "Lifting needs approval rights". A lifted recommendation can come back on the next read, unless a policy still stops it.

## Remove an order you already accepted

Where: [Supply Plan](/supply-planning/execution-plan) → the order's row → **Reject**, **Remove** or **Request removal**.

Removing an accepted order uses the same dialog and reasons. Where approval is required, **Request removal** keeps the order in the plan until an approver confirms. Some dialogs also offer **Just remove it**, which records no reason, so the plan may suggest it again.

## Recommendations from engine runs

Some organizations take their purchase, production or transfer orders from a recommendation engine instead of Tether's plan. On those pages:

- Table settings → **Recommendations** chooses whose suggestions show: **Tether's plan** or **Engine run**. Only one source shows, so no order counts twice.
- Chips such as "Purchase recs not updated since Nov 12" or "Purchase recs built on an older … run" warn that the engine has not re-run since the latest solve.

You accept and reject engine recommendations the same way. See [Engine runs](/supply-planning/engine-runs) for where each run comes from.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| A recommendation disappeared | Someone rejected it with **Not this time**, **Done another way** or **Never** | Check **Rejected** (Purchases) or **Dismissed** (Production, Transfers), then the **Never list** |
| A rejected recommendation came back early | Its shortfall got worse since the rejection | The row says "was rejected …; shortfall worse since". Reject it again if it still isn't needed |
| I rejected with **Wrong number** but it's still there | Wrong number, cost and date only flag the line for review | That's expected. Change the units and accept, or reject with another reason |
| No **Lift** button on the Never list | Lifting needs approval rights | Ask someone who approves plans |
| Lifted a Never and it still doesn't come back | A policy also stops it | Ask Tether to remove the policy |
| Accepted order isn't on the plan yet | Your organization requires approval | It waits under **Pending review** until approved on the [Supply Plan](/supply-planning/execution-plan) |
