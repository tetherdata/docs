**Where:** Supply Chain → **Series Explorer**.

Series Explorer draws one measure as a single timeline: what actually happened (history) up to today, and what the plan expects after it.

<Note>
Series Explorer is turned on per organization. If you don't see it under Supply Chain, ask Tether to turn it on. A link someone shares with you still opens it.
</Note>

## Chart inventory or supply over time

**Where:** Supply Chain → Series Explorer → toolbar filters.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what to chart">
    Pick a measure: **Inventory** (on-hand at the end of each period), **Production**, **Purchases** or **Transfers** (shipped from the location).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the selection">
    Filter by one or more SKU groups (required), and optionally by location group or location. Nothing is drawn until you choose a SKU group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the two sides">
    **Observed** sets which history to draw and **Planned** sets which plan to draw after today. Add a comparison line to draw a second plan beside it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The line under the pickers says where each half of the chart comes from. Click a point to see the rows behind it. The view is saved in the page address, so you can share or bookmark it, and save it as a view from the toolbar.

Series Explorer doesn't chart demand or sales yet. To see one SKU's inventory beside its demand week by week, use **Connected Planning** (Supply Chain → Connected Planning) or the [inventory history](/inventory/history).
