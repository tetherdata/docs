# Purchases

The Purchases page is the buying workbench: open POs already placed, the purchases you've committed, and the solver's suggested POs, organized by supplier and period. The page header shows the plan freshness indicator.

Where: Supply Chain → **Purchase**.

## Tabs

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Schedule** | The default buying workbench — purchases by supplier and period |
| **Worksheet** | The per-SKU inventory flow across the horizon |
| **List** | Every purchase by status: **Suggested**, **Pending review**, **Approved**, **Ordered**, **Rejected**. See [List views](#list-views) |

The active tab is URL-driven (`?tab=`), so deep links land on the right view. Each tab keeps its state (scroll, expansion, selection) when you switch away and back.

Organizations that require approval for purchases also see a **Review staged (N)** button. It opens the [Supply Plan](/supply-planning/execution-plan), where accepted buys are approved.

## List views

Where: Supply Chain → **Purchase** → **List** → the status switch at the top.

The List shows one status at a time. Each view has a help icon saying what it holds.

| View | What it holds | What you do there |
|------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Suggested** | Suggested buys not yet decided | Accept, change the units first, or reject with a reason. Saved lines move to **Pending review**. The **Order by** filter (7d, 14d, 30d, 90d, All) limits it to buys you must order soon. |
| **Pending review** | Accepted buys waiting for approval | Click an order to see what it covers, what it is for, and its rules. Approve on the [Supply Plan](/supply-planning/execution-plan). |
| **Approved** | Approved and not yet ordered | **Place order** creates the purchase order. |
| **Ordered** | Open PO lines not yet fully received, by the date each states | Grouped **Past due**, **Due in the next 2 weeks** and **Later**. |
| **Rejected** | Suggested buys someone rejected: who, when, why, and when each asks again | **Restore** puts one back under **Suggested**. |

A buy moves Suggested → Pending review (accepted) → Approved (approved on the Supply Plan) → Ordered (order placed). Without an approval requirement it skips Pending review. The List can be shown **Detailed** (one row per SKU line) or **By order** (lines rolled up into orders).

### Where do I see POs waiting for approval?

Where: Supply Chain → **Purchase** → **List** → **Pending review**, or Supply Chain → **Supply Plan** → **Purchases**.

### How do I see the buys someone rejected?

Where: Supply Chain → **Purchase** → **List** → **Rejected**. Rejected recommendations stay here until they ask again. Buys rejected with **Never** are on the Never list instead (Settings → Supply planning → Never list). See [Recommendations](/supply-planning/recommendations#stop-tether-from-recommending-something-ever-again).

### How do I turn an approved purchase into a real PO?

Where: Supply Chain → **Purchase** → **List** → **Approved** → **Place order**, or Supply Chain → **Supply Plan** → **Purchases** → **Place order**. To send POs to NetSuite or another ERP, see [Supply Plan](/supply-planning/execution-plan#send-approved-pos-to-netsuite-or-your-erp).

## Shared controls

The Worksheet and List share one view: filters (SKU, groups, type, supplier, BOM), the date range, granularity, Qty/$ display, and saved views all carry across the two tabs and round-trip through the URL. Table settings also offer:

- **Group by urgency** on the Worksheet (the List stays a flat triage grid).
- An **Anchor** control on the Schedule that re-buckets purchase dates by order date or arrival date.
- **Group by** on the Schedule: by supplier, by SKU group, by buy group, or by **SKU** alone. SKU lists every SKU as its own row with nothing collapsed, the way the Worksheet reads.
- **Export CSV** for the current rows.

## Why does the worksheet show the engine's buy instead of Tether's?

Where: Supply Chain → **Purchase** → **Worksheet** or **List** → **Table settings** → **Recommendations**.

If your organization takes purchase orders from a recommendation engine, the **Recommendations** setting chooses whose suggested buys show: **Tether's plan** or **Engine run**. Only one shows, so no buy counts twice. A chip such as "Purchase recs not updated since Nov 12" means the scheduled engine run didn't produce new recs for the latest solve. See [Engine runs](/supply-planning/engine-runs).

## Worksheet settings

Open **Table settings** on the Worksheet to change how its numbers read. Each setting shows a one-line explanation under its name, and the chosen option's explanation under its control.

| Setting | What it changes | Example |
|---------|-----------------|---------|
| **Recs only** | Shows only SKUs with a recommended purchase in the dates shown. | A SKU whose next buy falls after the last column is hidden. |
| **Shortfalls only** | Shows only SKUs whose stock goes negative, or below its coverage target, in the dates shown. | A SKU that dips to −20 in July stays; one that stays covered is hidden. |
| **Days of supply** | How the Days of supply figure is counted: stock carried forward week by week against the demand ahead of it, or month-end stock divided by that month's forecast per day. | 400 on hand with 100 a week of demand reads 28 days. 600 at month end against a forecast of 900 reads 20 days. |
| **Base position** | Which supply the Starting and Ending figures count. Each choice counts everything the one before it does, plus more. | **In progress** counts open POs, transfers and runs already under way; **Committed** adds the plan you've accepted. |
| **Recommendations** | Shown where your organization takes orders from an engine: whose recommended orders the table shows. The other source's are left out, so no order is counted twice. | **Tether's plan**: a buy Tether proposes for June shows in June. **Engine run**: the engine's June buy shows instead, and Tether's doesn't. |
| **Production use from** | How much of each component production uses up. It changes the figures for components, not finished goods. | **From the runs already committed**: a run of 1,000 bottles in May uses 1,000 caps in May. **Estimated from demand**: 500 bottles of demand in May uses 500 caps in May, run or not, and stock may go negative. |
| **Unapproved edits** | Whether edits nobody has approved yet count in the figures. | Typing 500 into June raises June's Ending by 500 under **In the base**; under **Hidden** the edit stays marked but June's Ending doesn't change until it is approved. |
| **Rec dates** | Which date a recommended purchase sits on. | A buy that must go out 3 May to land 14 June shows in June under **Landing date**, in May under **Order date**, and in both under **Both**. |

<Note>
Recommendations only appear under **From the runs already committed**. **Estimated from demand** shows no recommendations, so **Recs only** isn't offered there. Organizations that take orders from an engine open on **From the runs already committed**; others open on **Estimated from demand**.
</Note>

## The suggested PO details

Where: Supply Chain → **Purchase** → click a suggested PO (Schedule, Worksheet or List).

A suggested PO opens in the order editor, scoped to the SKU you clicked. From it you can:

- Change the units, then **Accept** the PO.
- **Reject** it with a reason. See [Rejecting a recommendation](/supply-planning/recommendations#reject-a-recommendation-with-a-reason).

The body shows the PO's line items, coverage impact ("Coverage on PO Land Date", see [Coverage](/supply-planning/coverage)), and supplier context.

### Round to MOQ or order multiple

When a suggested quantity is off the supplier's MOQ or order multiple, the editor shows **Off MOQ / multiple — round:** with two buttons: round down and round up to the nearest quantity the supplier accepts. Nothing changes until you pick one, and the quantity stays editable.

### Aggregate minimums (MOQ)

When a PO's items share an aggregate-minimum group, the drawer's **Aggregate minimums (MOQ)** section rolls them up: the group name, its member lines with quantities, the ordered total, and whether the floor is met. The minimum binds on the *summed* order across the group's SKUs — the rollup saves you from adding up per-SKU buys and guessing the floor.

## The locked PO drawer

Clicking a committed open PO (or a locked suggestion) opens a read-only summary drawer in the same visual language, with no action buttons. Its fact grid shows Supplier, PO number(s), Period, Order date, Arrival date, Lines, Total cost, and Total units, plus the per-line breakdown.

## From plan to order

Accepted purchases go to the [Supply Plan](/supply-planning/execution-plan). Where approval is required they wait under **Pending review** until approved. **Place order** then creates the purchase order, and placed POs appear under **Ordered** and on [Transactions](/transactions/overview).

## Best practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Work the Schedule by supplier, triage in the List">
    The Schedule groups the buy by supplier and period — the shape a PO actually takes. The List is best for a cross-supplier urgency pass; sort by urgency or value, then open drawers to act.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Check the MOQ rollup before trimming lines">
    Removing or shrinking one line can drop the group's summed order below its aggregate minimum, invalidating the whole PO. The rollup's met/not-met state tells you how much slack you have.
  </Accordion>


  <Accordion title="Use the anchor to match your calendar">
    Bucketing the Schedule by order date builds this week's work queue; bucketing by arrival date shows the receiving calendar. Same data, different question.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| A suggested PO opens as a read-only summary | Its recommendation was rejected or dropped by the latest solve | Check **List** → **Rejected**; the summary still shows the PO's contents |
| Modify doesn't change the suggestion | Editing always operates on the committed PO, not the live suggestion | Accept first (or accept the line), then edit the committed movement |
| MOQ section missing from a drawer | None of the PO's items carry an aggregate-minimum group | Nothing to roll up — per-line minimums still apply through the order form's rounding |
| **Review staged** missing | Your organization doesn't require approval for purchases | Accepted buys are approved straight away |
| A buy I rejected is gone from **Rejected** | It reached its ask-again date, or was rejected with **Never** | Check the Never list in Settings → Supply planning |
| Grouping control missing on the List | Urgency grouping applies to the Worksheet only | The List stays flat by design; use column sort instead |
