# Reports and analytics

Tether has no single "Reports" menu. Reporting lives where the work is. Use this page to find the right place.

## Is there a reports page?

**Where:** depends on what you need; most organizations use **Agent** → **Standing reports** and **Inventory** → **Custom views**.

| You want… | Go to |
|-----------|-------|
| A live summary of service level, stock risk and open orders | **Overview** (if your organization has one). See [Overview](/analytics/overview). |
| A report that runs on a schedule (for example, a Monday stock-risk summary) | **Agent** → **Standing reports**. Ask the agent to set one up in chat; this page lists each report, when it runs, and what the last run said, and lets you change its schedule. |
| A report built for your team (such as a daily DTC report) | **Inventory** → **Custom views**, or a view Tether has placed in your sidebar |
| Forecast accuracy | **Demand** → **Comparison** → **Accuracy / vs Actuals** (below) |
| A one-off number or list | Ask the agent in chat |
| A table you can open in Excel | Any table's **Table Settings** → **Export CSV**. See [Exporting data](/tips/exports). |

## Where can I see forecast accuracy?

**Where:** **Demand** → **Comparison** → **Accuracy / vs Actuals** tab.

The **Accuracy / vs Actuals** tab compares forecasts with what actually sold. It shows **MAPE** (average percentage error), **Bias (signed)** (whether you over- or under-forecast), **Coverage** (how much of actual demand had a forecast), and **FVA vs naïve** (whether your forecast beats a simple naïve forecast), with a trend over time and a heatmap of where the errors are. The **Compare forecasts** tab sets two forecasts side by side. See [Forecast comparison](/demand-forecasting/comparison).

## How do I see inventory turns?

**Where:** ask the agent, or a custom view.

Tether has no built-in inventory-turns or fill-rate screen. Ask the agent, for example "What were inventory turns by SKU group last quarter?", and it calculates from your sales and inventory history. If your team needs it every week, ask the agent to make it a standing report, or ask Tether about a custom view.

For days of cover and run-out dates, see [Coverage and run-out](/inventory/coverage-and-runout).

## Metrics Explorer

**Where:** go to `/metrics` in your browser (for example `app.tetherdata.io/metrics`). It isn't in the sidebar or search.

**Metrics Explorer** lists stored metric values (such as sales units, revenue and average price) by date. Choose a **Metric**, **Channel**, SKU group, **SKU**, **Start Date** and **End Date**. Cards show **Total Value**, **Data Points** and **Edited Values** (manual adjustments), and a table lists each value. Pick a date range first; until you do, it says "Please select a date range to view metrics". It needs metrics access; if you're sent to an access-denied page, ask an Admin.

## Finance

If your organization has the **Finance** section turned on, it is a preview with sample data. To record supplier bills and payments for real, see [Supplier invoices and payments](/finance/overview).
