# User roles & permissions

Each person in your organization has one or more roles. A role is a bundle of permissions, and permissions decide which sidebar pages, tabs and buttons you see. When you lack a permission, Tether hides the control instead of showing a button that would fail.

## What roles are there?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Access** → **Members**, in the **Role** column.

| Role | Who it's for | What it can do |
|------|--------------|----------------|
| **Admin** | Planners, operations and inventory managers, whoever runs Tether for your team | Works across the app: products, inventory, demand, supply planning, orders, integrations, and most of Settings, including inviting people and changing roles. |
| **Sales Rep** | Sales and account teams | Sales-focused access: sales history, demand forecasts and channel information. Can be limited to certain channels with [channel access rules](#how-do-i-restrict-a-sales-rep-to-certain-channels). |
| **Receiving** | Warehouse staff who check in deliveries | Receives inbound orders and little else. See [What can the Receiving role do?](#what-can-the-receiving-role-do) |
| **Tether Admin** | Tether's own support staff | Everything an Admin can do, plus internal setup pages. You can't invite someone as Tether Admin. |

You can invite people as **Admin**, **Sales Rep** or **Receiving**. A person can hold more than one role. The exact permissions in each role are managed by Tether; if a role needs to see something it can't, ask Tether.

## What can the Receiving role do?

**Where:** **Transactions** → **Transactions**.

The Receiving role is a locked-down role for people who record what physically arrives. It can:

- Open an inbound order (such as a purchase order) and set its line items.
- Enter the quantities received and complete the receipt.

It can't delete or archive orders, change costs or add cost items, adjust inventory directly, or act on the supply plan.

On organizations that record stock movements in Tether's movement ledger, the **Transactions** page has a **Receiving** tab. Someone who can receive but can't create orders opens straight onto **Receiving** and sees only that tab, without the order list, **Import orders** or **New order**.

## Why can't I see the Approvals tab?

**Where:** **Inventory** → **Dashboard** → **Approvals** tab.

Tabs on the inventory page depend on what your role can do:

| Tab | Shown when you can… |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Inventory**, **History** | read inventory |
| **Counts** | count stock, and your organization uses the movement ledger |
| **Approvals** | approve inventory adjustments. Approvers' own changes apply immediately. |
| **My requests** | propose inventory adjustments that wait for an approver |

If you need to approve adjustments, ask an Admin to give you a role that includes approval, or ask Tether.

### The stock-counting view

Some organizations give warehouse counters a stripped-down inventory view. With it, the inventory table shows only what's needed to count, and **Upload Inventory** is hidden, so counts go through review instead of overwriting on-hand directly. Ask Tether if you want it for a role.

## Why don't I see Settings?

**Where:** the **Settings** link at the bottom of the sidebar, or the **Tether** workspace menu.

Settings appears when your role can open at least one settings page besides **Profile** and **Theme**. It isn't only for Admins: each Settings entry has its own permission, so different roles see different entries. If you see no Settings link, your role has no settings pages; you can still change **Profile** and **Theme** from the **Tether** workspace menu.

## Why can't I see a sidebar page?

Your role, your organization's enabled features, and your organization's sidebar setup all decide what shows. See [Why don't I see a page in my sidebar?](/navigation#why-dont-i-see-a-page-in-my-sidebar) for the full list of sections and who sees each.

## How do I change someone's role?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Access** → **Members** → **Members** tab → the person's **Role** cell.

Admins click the role to open **Edit roles**, tick or untick roles under **Assignable roles**, and click **Save**. Changing your own roles asks you to confirm first, and Tether won't let you remove the last Admin. See [Members and invitations](/administration/user-access) for inviting and removing people.

## How do I restrict a sales rep to certain channels?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Access** → **Channel Access** → click the person → **Access Rules** → **Add Rule**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check channel access is on">
    Open **Settings** → **Access** → **Channel Access**. If it says "All users can see all channels (no restrictions)", channel access isn't turned on for your organization. Ask Tether to turn it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the person">
    Click the person in the list. Their **User Access** page shows their role and email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a rule">
    Under **Access Rules**, click **Add Rule**. For **Criteria Type**, pick **Specific Channels** (choose channels one by one) or **Hierarchy Level** (grant every channel under a level of your channel hierarchy, such as a region). Click **Create Rule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Once channel access is on, sales reps see only the channels they've been granted. A sales rep with no rules sees no channels.
</Warning>
