# Lead Time Configuration

Lead time is the number of days from placing an order until the stock is available at the destination. Tether uses it to decide when to order or transfer so stock arrives before you run out, and to date incoming stock in projections.

There are two kinds:

- **Supplier lead time**: from placing a purchase order to receiving it.
- **Transit lead time**: days to move stock between two of your locations.

Where you set them depends on your setup. If your sidebar has **Supply Chain** → **Locations**, use the first two sections. If it has **Inventory** → **Warehouses** and **Inventory** → **Suppliers**, use the older-setup sections.

## Where do I change a supplier's lead time? (Locations)

Where: **Supply Chain** → **Locations** → the supplier → **Purchasing**.

- For everything bought from this supplier: under **Purchasing Defaults**, set **Lead** (days).
- For one SKU: set the lead time on that SKU's row in the same tab. A SKU-level lead time applies to that SKU instead of the default.

Both are saved as policies, so they also appear on the location's **Policies** tab. See [Locations](/supply-chain/locations#how-do-i-see-a-suppliers-purchasing-terms).

## How do I set transit days between two warehouses? (Locations)

Where: **Supply Chain** → **Locations** → a location → **Overview** → **Connections**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the connection">
    On **Connections**, find the lane to the other location and edit its lead days. If there is no lane yet, click **Add Connection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set direction and days">
    For a new connection choose **Receives from** or **Ships to**, pick the other location, and enter the days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the row. Tether stores the days as a policy named **Lead time: [other location]** for that one direction. Set the reverse direction separately if it differs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why is the lead time on my PO wrong?

Where: the supplier's **Purchasing** tab and the location's **Policies** tab (Locations setup), or the supplier's **Overview** capabilities table (older setup).

A purchase order's expected arrival comes from the lead time in effect for that supplier, SKU and destination when the order was planned. If it looks wrong:

1. Check the supplier's **Purchasing** tab (Locations setup) or **Overview** capabilities table (older setup): is there a SKU-level lead time that differs from the default?
2. Check the location's **Policies** tab for another lead-time policy that applies to this SKU or lane.
3. After you correct the lead time, new recommendations use it. Orders already placed keep the date they were given; change that on the order itself.

## Transit lead times between warehouses (older setup)

Where: **Inventory** → **Warehouses** → a warehouse → **Overview** → **Transit Lead Times to Other Warehouses**.

The table lists every other warehouse with **Destination Warehouse** and **Lead Time (Days)**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the destination">
    Locate the destination warehouse in the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter transit days">
    Type the number of days in **Lead Time (Days)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update**, press **Enter**, or click away (**Cancel** reverts). The value is saved for both directions: setting NYC → LA also sets LA → NYC.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supplier lead times (older setup)

Where: **Inventory** → **Suppliers** → a supplier → **Overview** → capabilities table → **Lead Time (days)**.

Each SKU a supplier provides has its own lead time. Click the value, enter the days, and save. See [Suppliers](/inventory/suppliers#where-do-i-set-moq-or-lead-time-for-a-supplier).

If the supplier holds stock for you in a linked warehouse, its **Lead Times** tab sets transit days from that warehouse to your others, using the same table as a warehouse's Overview.

## What to include in a lead time

Count the whole time from order to usable stock: order processing, manufacturing (if made to order), shipping, customs, and receiving. If a route is unreliable, use the longer end of what you see. Tether also raises a [data issue](/supply-planning/data-issues) when a location's replenishment lead time is as long as or longer than the cover it is meant to hold.
