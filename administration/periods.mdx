# Period management (fiscal calendars)

By default Tether groups dates into calendar weeks and months. If your business reports in its own fiscal periods, such as a 4-4-5 calendar, upload a period mapping and turn on custom time periods. Tether then labels and groups dates by your periods.

## Where do I change period settings?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Forecasting** → **Period Management**.

The page has three tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **Overview** | **System Status**: whether **Custom Time Aggregation** is on, the **Active Mapping**, its **Total Days**, **Date Range** and number of **Periods**. **Quick Actions**: **Upload New Period Mapping**, **Refresh Data**. |
| **Configuration** | The **Enable Custom Time Periods** switch, the current mapping and its periods, and **Display Preferences** (how period and fiscal-year labels look) |
| **Mappings** | Your uploaded **Period Mappings**, and **Upload New Mapping** |

Uploading and switching need an Admin-level role; others can view the page.

## How do I set up a 4-4-5 fiscal calendar?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Forecasting** → **Period Management** → **Mappings** → **Upload New Mapping**.

There is no fiscal-year-start or week-start setting. Instead you give Tether a file that says which fiscal period every date belongs to, so any calendar works: 4-4-5, 4-5-4, 13 periods, or a fiscal year starting in any month.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the CSV">
    One row per calendar day, with these three columns:

    | Column | Example | Meaning |
    |--------|---------|---------|
    | `calendar_date` | `2026-02-01` | The date, as YYYY-MM-DD |
    | `period_label` | `P01` | The fiscal period that date falls in |
    | `fiscal_year` | `FY2026` | The fiscal year it falls in |

    For a 4-4-5 calendar, the first 28 days get `P01`, the next 28 `P02`, the next 35 `P03`, and so on. Cover every date you plan and report on, past and future.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload it">
    On the **Mappings** tab, click **Upload New Mapping**. In **Upload Period Mapping**, choose the file. Tether checks it and lists any errors by row, such as a missing column or a date not in YYYY-MM-DD form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    On the **Configuration** tab, turn on **Enable Custom Time Periods**. The switch only works once a mapping has been uploaded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it">
    On **Overview**, **System Status** should show the mapping as active with the date range and number of periods you expect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do I upload our fiscal periods?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Forecasting** → **Period Management** → **Mappings** → **Upload New Mapping** (or **Overview** → **Upload New Period Mapping**).

Follow the steps above. To change your calendar later, upload a new mapping the same way.

## Why can't I see Period Management?

Your role doesn't include it. Ask an Admin.
