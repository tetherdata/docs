# Members and invitations

Admins manage who belongs to your organization in **Settings** → **Access** → **Members**. The page has two tabs: **Members** (people who have joined) and **Invitations** (people you've invited who haven't joined yet). Older versions of these docs called this the "User Access card"; there is no such card.

## How do I invite a new user?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Access** → **Members** → **Invitations** tab → **Invite User**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Invitations">
    Go to **Settings** → **Members** and click the **Invitations** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite User">
    In the **Invite User** dialog, enter their **Email** and pick a **Role**: **Admin**, **Sales Rep** or **Receiving**. See [User roles & permissions](/roles-permissions) for what each can do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to send it">
    Leave **Send email** on to email them the invitation. Turn it off to get an invitation link instead: the **Invitation Created** dialog shows the URL with a copy button, so you can send it yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send Invitation**. The invitation appears in the **Invitations** list until they accept it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## My invite expired, what do I do?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Access** → **Members** → **Invitations** tab.

The **Status** column shows when each invitation expires, or an **Expired** badge once it has. An expired invitation can't be used. Revoke it (the row's **Revoke** action) and click **Invite User** to send a new one to the same email.

## How do I change someone's role?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Access** → **Members** → **Members** tab → **Role** column.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click their role">
    In the **Members** tab, click the person's role. The **Edit roles** popover opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick the roles they should have">
    Under **Assignable roles**, tick or untick **Admin**, **Sales Rep** and **Receiving**. A person can hold more than one. **Managed roles** such as Tether Admin are shown but can't be changed here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. You'll see "Role updated".
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you change your own roles, Tether asks you to confirm in **Change Your Own Roles**, because you could lose access to this page. Tether won't let you remove the Admin role from the only Admin in the organization.

## How do I remove a user?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Access** → **Members** → **Members** tab → the person's **Remove** action.

Choose **Remove** on their row and confirm in **Remove Member**. They lose access to your organization right away. This can't be undone; to bring them back, invite them again. You can't remove the only Admin.

There is no "deactivate" or "reactivate": removing is the only way to take someone's access away. Passwords and sign-in are handled by your login provider, not in Tether's Settings.

## How do I limit what a sales rep sees?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Access** → **Channel Access** → click the person → **Access Rules**.

Channel access rules decide which channels a sales rep can see. See [How do I restrict a sales rep to certain channels?](/roles-permissions#how-do-i-restrict-a-sales-rep-to-certain-channels)

## Why can't I see Members?

The **Members** entry only shows for roles that can read the member list, usually Admins. If you can see members but there's no **Invite User** button, no **Remove** action, or roles can't be clicked, your role can view members but not change them.
