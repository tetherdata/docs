# Settings reference

Settings opens from the **Settings** link at the bottom of the sidebar, or from the **Tether** workspace menu. Each entry has its own permission, so you see only the ones your role can open. Entries marked *staff* are for Tether's team. Entries marked *if turned on* appear only for organizations that have that feature. "Admins" below means roles granted that page, which is usually the Admin role; if you need one you can't see, ask an Admin or Tether.

## Where do I set default columns for everyone?

**Where:** **Settings** → **General** → **Default Columns**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the table">
    Choose the **Table** (for example orders, purchases, sales orders or shipment lines).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose and order columns">
    Tick each column to show by default, and use the arrows to set the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save organization default**. Everyone starts with these columns. People who already saved their own column view keep it. **Remove default** puts everyone back on Tether's standard columns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Saving needs an Admin-level role.

## How do I add a pallet or case unit?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Catalog** → **Units** → **Add unit**.

**Units** is your organization's catalog of containers, such as truck, pallet, case and carton. The **Unit catalog** lists each unit with what it's **Counted as**, its **Capacity** (how many of another unit fit inside, for example 26 pallets per truck), **Weight (kg)** and where it's **Used by**. Click **Add unit** to add one. **Default shipment units** sets which units shipments are counted in by default.

## Where do I set units per case?

**Where:** **Products** → **SKUs** → a SKU → **Units** tab → **Add conversion**.

Units per case belongs to each SKU, not to Settings. On the SKU's **Units** tab, click **Add conversion**, pick the **Unit** (for example a case from your unit catalog), and enter the **Ratio**: the number of base units in one of that unit. A case of 12 has a ratio of 12. You can also enter the case's volume (CBM) and weight (kg), which shipments use to count cartons, volume and weight.

## Where do I set up working days?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Catalog** → **Working-days Calendars**.

The page has an **Organization calendar** (your workdays and holidays) and per-location overrides for production locations. It feeds production scheduling.

<Warning>
  This page is a preview: it shows "Preview: changes are local-only", meaning what you enter is not saved yet. Ask Tether before relying on it.
</Warning>

## Where do I set working days for a supplier?

**Where:** nowhere yet.

Tether has no working-days calendar for suppliers. **Working-days Calendars** covers your organization and production locations only (and is a preview). To account for a supplier's shutdown, adjust its lead time or tell your planner; ask Tether if you need supplier calendars.

## How do I change what's on our PO PDF?

**Where:** **Settings** → **General** → **Documents** → **Templates**.

1. Set **Document type** to purchase orders and pick the **Template** (the default is marked "(default)"), or click **New template** or **Duplicate**.
2. **Branding** sets your letterhead: legal name, logo (PNG), address, contact details, accent color and font.
3. **Line-item columns**: tick **Print** for each column to show and set its heading and order. **Header fields**: tick **Print** for each field, or **Add custom field**. **Sections**: totals, charges, line memos, signature lines and footer text.
4. Check the **Live preview** and click **Save**. **Make default** makes a template the one orders use.

To make one kind of order always print with a particular template, set **Prints as** on it in **Settings** → **Catalog** → **Transaction types**.

## General

| Entry | What it does | Who sees it |
|-------|--------------|-------------|
| **Profile** | Your account, your organization, and sign out | Everyone |
| **Theme** | **Color Theme** and **Dark Mode** | Everyone |
| **Terminology** | Rename Tether's words (SKU Group, Location, Policy and more) across the app, with a preview. See [Terminology](/administration/terminology). | Admins |
| **Documents** | Document templates for order PDFs and CSVs: letterhead, columns, header fields, sections (above). | Admins |
| **Default Columns** | Your organization's default table columns (above) | Admins |

## Catalog

| Entry | What it does | Who sees it |
|-------|--------------|-------------|
| **Collection Hierarchy** | Hierarchy fields for collections; being retired in favour of Attributes | Older organizations not on Products |
| **SKU Group Hierarchy** | Up to five levels that classify SKU groups (named for your organization's word for SKU groups) | Admins |
| **Channel Hierarchy** | Hierarchy fields for channels (such as region), used for reporting and [channel access rules](/roles-permissions#how-do-i-restrict-a-sales-rep-to-certain-channels) | Admins |
| **Channel Bindings** | Maps the channel codes your systems send to your channels, and lists **Unresolved values** that matched nothing | Admins |
| **Working-days Calendars** | Organization workdays and holidays (preview, above) | Admins |
| **Units** | Unit catalog: trucks, pallets, cases (above) | Admins |
| **Transaction types** | Your order types: name, what kind of order each is, default origin and destination, Incoterms, usual shipping mode, which template it **Prints as**, and whether it ships in full | People who can edit orders; *if turned on* |
| **Shipments** | Whether orders follow their shipment's status, and whether search matches by SKU only | People who can edit orders; *if turned on* |
| **Sales orders** | **Build by** (days before the requested delivery), **Automatic lots**, and whether **Printing reserves lots** | People who can edit orders |
| **Production** | How production runs are recorded: staging and leftovers, how inputs start, loss reasons, lot codes | Admins |
| **Lot Tracking** | Which SKUs capture lot codes, and shelf-life defaults | *If turned on* |
