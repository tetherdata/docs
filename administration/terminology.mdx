# Terminology

Tether lets your organization rename the words it uses for things, so the app speaks your vocabulary. Rename **SKU Group** to **Style** and every page, menu, filter and sidebar item that says SKU Group says Style instead.

## Why does my sidebar say Styles instead of SKU Groups?

An administrator in your organization renamed the term. Sidebar items, page titles, column headers and buttons all follow your organization's terminology, so your app may say **Styles**, **Recipes** or **Plants** where these docs say **SKU Groups**, **BOMs** or **Locations**. The pages work the same; only the words differ.

## How do I rename a term?

Where: Settings → **Terminology** (page title **Terminology Settings**).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Terminology">
    In **Settings**, click **Terminology**. Terms are grouped into sections: **Catalog**, **Network**, **Policies**, **Demand**, **Inventory** and **Planning views**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the term">
    Find the term under **Entity** and type your word in **Singular** and **Plural** (for example Style / Styles). **Internal Notes** is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Preview">
    The **Preview** card shows sample catalog and network labels with your new words.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes** at the top of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To undo one term, use **Reset** on its row. To undo every change, click **Reset All** and confirm; this can't be undone.

You see this page with the `settings:terminology:access` permission. Saving Catalog, Network, Policies and Planning views terms also needs `terminology:write`; Demand and Inventory terms need `actions:execute`. See [Roles and permissions](/roles-permissions).

## Which terms can I rename?

| Section | Terms |
|---------|-------|
| **Catalog** | SKU, SKU Group, Primary SKU Group, Secondary SKU Group, Attribute, Attribute Value, BOM, Component, Planning Group, Variant Preference, Sub Type, Unit Conversion, External ID |
| **Network** | Location, Location Group, Production Line, Production Run, Supplier, Lane, Capability |
| **Policies** | Policy, Scope, Declaration, Condition |
| **Demand** | Channel, Channel Group, Forecast |
| **Inventory** | Inventory, Purchase Order, Ex-factory, and the names of the days-of-supply rules (Tether's projection, Pooled walk, MRP month-end, Flat rate, Weekly walk) |
| **Planning views** | The supply plan's view names: Do nothing, Follow my plan, Tether's plan, No input limits, Committed production, Estimated from demand, Engine run. Here the two fields are **Control label** (what the button says) and **Dataset name** (what the data under it is called). |

### Words that follow another term

- **Product** and **Collection** follow **SKU Group**. Rename SKU Group and they change with it.
- **Warehouse** follows **Location**.

These aren't listed separately.

### Words that can't be renamed

- The **Products** section label in the sidebar. It covers SKUs, SKU groups, BOMs and attributes, so it isn't tied to any one term.
- The SKU type names: Finished Good, Variant, Bundle, Kit, Virtual Bundle, Component.
- The cost and price names on a SKU's **Cost & price** tab (Default cost, Standard cost, Sales price…), except that **Supplier price** and **Inventory cost** follow your Supplier and Inventory terms. See [Cost & price](/principal-catalog/cost-and-price).

## Examples

| Industry | Change |
|----------|--------|
| Apparel | SKU Group → Style, Attribute → Option |
| Food and beverage | BOM → Recipe, Component → Ingredient, Production Run → Batch |
| Manufacturing | Location → Plant, BOM → Formula |
| Retail | Channel → Banner, Supplier → Vendor |
