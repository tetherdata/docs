# Navigation & interface

Everything in Tether is reached from the left sidebar. There is no top navigation bar. Which sections and pages you see depends on your role, your organization's enabled features, and how Tether has set up your organization's sidebar.

## What's at the top of the sidebar?

**Where:** the top of the left sidebar.

- **Tether** (workspace menu): click it to see your name and role, switch **Sidebar** between **Full**, **Compact** and **Hidden**, switch **Mode** between **Light** and **Dark**, and open **Profile**, **Theme**, **Settings** or log out.
- **New chat**: starts a new conversation with the agent.
- **Search** (`⌘ + K`, or `Ctrl + K` on Windows): opens the [command palette](/tips/command-palette).

## Where is Settings?

**Where:** the **Settings** link at the bottom of the sidebar, or **Tether** (workspace menu) → **Settings**.

Settings appears when your role can open at least one settings page beyond **Profile** and **Theme**. It is not limited to Admins: for example, on older organizations Sales Reps can open **Inventory & Forecast** to edit channel availability, so they see Settings too. If you see only Profile and Theme in the workspace menu, your role has no settings pages. See [Settings reference](/administration/settings-reference) for every entry.

## Sidebar sections and pages

**Where:** the left sidebar. This is the full list; you'll see a subset.

| Section | Pages | Who sees it |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| **Overview** | Overview | Only when Tether has set up an Overview for your organization. See [Overview](/analytics/overview). |
| **Agent** | Chat Sessions, Standing reports | Anyone who can use the agent. |
| **Demand** | Dashboard, Comparison, Season Plan, Hybrid Demand Plan, Edit Log, Admin | Season Plan and Hybrid Demand Plan only when turned on for your organization. Admin only for forecast admins. |
| **Marketing & Promotions** | Drivers, Promotions, Price Calendar | Only when Marketing & Promotions is turned on for your organization. |
| **Finance** | Dashboard, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, General Ledger, Reports, Profitability, Budget & Forecast | Only when turned on for your organization (a preview with sample data). See [Supplier invoices and payments](/finance/overview). |
| **Inventory** | Dashboard, Locations (or Warehouses), Suppliers, Lots, Custom views, plus any custom views placed here | Locations/Warehouses and Suppliers only on organizations not yet on Locations (see below). Lots only with lot tracking. Custom views for anyone allowed to read them. |
| **Transactions** | Transactions, Review lists, Shipments | Review lists and Shipments only when turned on for your organization. |
| **Supply Chain** | Locations, Policies, Connected Planning, Series Explorer, Transfer, Production, Purchase, MRP, Supply Plan | Organizations on the supply network. Planning pages need planning access. Series Explorer only when turned on. |
| **Products** | SKUs, SKU Groups, BOMs, Attributes | Organizations on the Products catalog (most live customers). Formerly called "Principal Catalog". |
| **Catalog** | Collections, SKUs | Older organizations not on Products. |
| **Sales** | Sales Channels, Orders, Sales History | Sales Channels disappears once channels move to Locations. Orders needs sales-order access. |

<Note>
  Labels such as Locations, Policies, SKUs, SKU Groups, BOMs and Attributes follow your organization's names in **Settings** → **Terminology**, so your sidebar may use different words.
</Note>

### What changed from older versions of these docs

**Where:** the left sidebar and **Settings**.

- **Command Center** is now **Overview**.
- **Principal Catalog** (Attribute, SKU, SKU Group, BOM) is now **Products** (SKUs, SKU Groups, BOMs, Attributes).
- **Execution plan** is now **Supply Plan**.
- **Memory** is no longer in the Agent section; it has **Standing reports** instead.
- **Data issues** moved out of Supply Chain to **Settings** → **Supply planning** → **Data issues**.
- **Sales Orders** is now **Sales** → **Orders**.

## Why don't I see a page in my sidebar?

**Where:** the left sidebar.

A page is missing for one of three reasons:

1. **It isn't turned on for your organization.** Shipments, Review lists, Season Plan, Hybrid Demand Plan, Series Explorer, Lots, Marketing & Promotions and Finance are only shown to organizations that have them. Ask Tether to turn it on.
2. **Your role can't open it.** For example, Demand → Admin needs forecast-admin access, and Orders needs sales-order access. Ask an Admin to change your role in [Members](/administration/user-access).
3. **Your organization's sidebar hides or moves it.** See the next section.

Older pages also disappear when your organization moves to Locations: **Inventory → Warehouses** and **Inventory → Suppliers** give way to **Supply Chain → Locations**, and **Sales → Sales Channels** gives way to channel locations.

## Can we rename, hide or reorder sidebar sections?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Navigation** (Tether staff only).

Yes. Tether can set, for your whole organization, which sections, pages and Settings entries are shown, move a page to another section, change their order, and add sections of your own (for example, a section that holds your custom views). Ask Tether to change it. To rename what things are called (for example, "SKU Groups" to "Styles"), an Admin uses **Settings** → **Terminology**.

Each person can also collapse a section by clicking its heading, or switch the whole sidebar to **Compact** or **Hidden** from the **Tether** workspace menu.

## Settings menu

**Where:** **Settings**. Settings has its own sidebar, grouped like this. You see only the entries your role allows.

| Group | Entries |
|-------|---------|
| **General** | Profile, Theme, Terminology, Documents, Default Columns |
| **Catalog** | Collection Hierarchy, SKU Group Hierarchy, Channel Hierarchy, Channel Bindings, Working-days Calendars, Units, Transaction types, Shipments, Sales orders, Production, Lot Tracking |
| **Forecasting** | Forecast Admin, Inventory & Forecast, Period Management, Forecast Edit Log, Order Grouping, Automatic Re-planning |
| **Integrations** | Data Ingestion, BigQuery, API Keys, MCP |
| **Agent** | Agent Settings, Agent Files |
| **Access** | Channel Access, Members |
| **Administration** | Setup & Health, Principal Setup & Health, ERP SKU ID Sync, Mapper Config, Data Management, Supply planning, Reconciliation, Navigation, Feature Flags, Inventory Sources, Organizations |

What each entry does is in [Settings reference](/administration/settings-reference).

## Tabs on a page

Many pages split into tabs, and the selected tab is kept in the URL, so you can bookmark or share a link to it. For example, **Inventory** → **Dashboard** has **Inventory**, **History**, **Counts**, **Approvals** and **My requests**. Counts, Approvals and My requests only show when your role can count stock, approve adjustments or propose them.

## Tables

**Where:** the **Table Settings** button (or sliders icon) above most tables.

**Table Settings** holds the table's options: which columns show, grouping, row density, **Show all filters**, **Hide 0s**, and **Export CSV**, depending on the table. See [Exports](/tips/exports) and [Keyboard shortcuts](/tips/shortcuts).
