# Supplier invoices and payments

You record supplier bills and payments in Tether by asking the agent. Tether then matches each bill against the purchase order and what you received (a three-way match) and tells you what doesn't line up.

## Where do I record a supplier invoice?

**Where:** **New chat** (top of the sidebar) → ask the agent.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell the agent about the bill">
    For example: "Record invoice INV-2207 from Acme for PO 1042: 500 units of SKU TEE-BLK-M at $4.10, plus $180 freight." You can attach the invoice file to the chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preview">
    The agent shows a preview of every bill and line before saving: which order line each charge applies to, the quantity billed, and any freight or duty charge, which goes to that shipment's landed cost.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Approve the preview to record it. The agent reports each bill's total and open balance, and warns you if it looks like a duplicate of an earlier bill.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do I record a payment to a supplier?

**Where:** **New chat** → ask the agent.

Tell the agent what you paid and which bills it covers, for example "We paid Acme $2,230 on 12 Nov for INV-2207." One payment can settle several bills; anything it doesn't settle is held on account. This records that you paid; it doesn't move money or connect to your bank.

## How do I see invoice discrepancies?

**Where:** **New chat** → ask "Which supplier invoices don't match?"

The agent lists, per purchase order line:

- **Quantity variance**: billed more than received.
- **Price variance**: billed at a different unit price.
- **Invoiced, not received** and **received, not invoiced**.
- **Currency mismatch**.

It also flags bills with a problem of their own (a suspected duplicate, an amount not allocated to any line, a freight or duty charge with no shipment), and totals goods received but not invoiced per supplier. Ask for a date, such as "as of 31 October", to see it as it stood at month end.

## What is the Finance section?

**Where:** sidebar → **Finance** (only if turned on for your organization).

**Finance** (Dashboard, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, General Ledger, Reports, Profitability, Budget & Forecast) is a preview that shows sample data, not your organization's figures. Use the agent, as above, to record real bills and payments.
