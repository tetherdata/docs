# Quickstart

This page walks you through your first session in Tether. Most of your data usually arrives from your connected systems (Shopify, NetSuite, your 3PL and others), so setup is mostly checking what came in and filling gaps.

<Note>
  Tether has two versions of the catalog. **Most organizations** use **Products** (SKUs, SKU Groups, BOMs, Attributes) and **Supply Chain** → **Locations**. **Older organizations** use **Catalog** (Collections, SKUs) and **Inventory** → **Warehouses**. Look at your sidebar: if you see a **Products** section, follow the Products steps.
</Note>

## Sign in and find your way around

Go to [app.tetherdata.io](https://app.tetherdata.io) and sign in with the account you were invited with. Everything is in the left sidebar; press `⌘ + K` (`Ctrl + K` on Windows) to search for any page, SKU or order. See [Navigation & interface](/navigation) for the full sidebar map.

## How do I set up my first SKUs?

**Where:** **Products** → **SKUs** → **Create SKU** (most organizations). Older organizations: **Catalog** → **SKUs** → **Create SKU**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Products (most organizations)">
    1. Open **Products** → **Attributes** and check the attributes your products vary by (such as size or color). Click **New attribute** to add one.
    2. Open **Products** → **SKU Groups** and click **New sku group** for a family of related SKUs (a style, a product line).
    3. Open **Products** → **SKUs** and click **Create SKU**, or open a SKU group and use **Bulk Create SKUs** to create every variant at once.
    4. If a SKU is made from components, add its bill of materials in **Products** → **BOMs** → **New BOM**.

    See [Products overview](/principal-catalog/overview) for details.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Catalog (older organizations)">
    1. Open **Catalog** → **Collections** and click **New Collection**.
    2. Open the collection and add SKUs with **Add SKU**, or go to **Catalog** → **SKUs** → **Create SKU**.

    See [SKUs overview](/product-catalog/skus/overview).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

If your SKUs come from a connected system, they appear here after the first sync; you don't need to create them by hand.

## How do I add a new warehouse?

**Where:** **Supply Chain** → **Locations** → **+ Location** (most organizations). Older organizations: **Inventory** → **Warehouses** → **New Warehouse**.

- **Organizations on Locations:** warehouses, suppliers, factories and sales channels are all locations. Open **Supply Chain** → **Locations** and click **+ Location**. The old Warehouses page sends you here automatically.
- **Older organizations:** open **Inventory** → **Warehouses** (your sidebar may say **Locations**, depending on your organization's terminology) and click **New Warehouse**.

## Where do I add a sales channel?

**Where:** **Sales** → **Sales Channels** (older organizations) or **Supply Chain** → **Locations** (organizations on Locations).

There is no "Create Channel" button. Sales channels come from your supply network and connected sales systems: when you connect Shopify, Amazon or another sales source, its channels appear. An empty list says "Sales channels arrive from the supply network". If a channel is missing, check the integration in **Settings** → **Integrations** → **Data Ingestion**, or ask Tether.

## Connect your systems

**Where:** **Settings** → **Integrations** → **Data Ingestion**.

Integrations bring in your SKUs, inventory, orders and sales. See [Integration setup](/integrations/setup).

## Check your inventory

**Where:** **Inventory** → **Dashboard**.

The **Inventory** tab shows stock by SKU and location. Other tabs: **History** (every change), **Counts** (physical counts), **Approvals** (adjustments waiting for you) and **My requests** (adjustments you proposed). Counts, Approvals and My requests show only if your role uses them. Admins can load stock from a spreadsheet with **Upload Inventory**. See [Inventory dashboard](/inventory/dashboard).

## How do I invite a teammate?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Access** → **Members** → **Invitations** tab → **Invite User**.

Enter their **Email**, choose a **Role** (**Admin**, **Sales Rep** or **Receiving**) and click **Send Invitation**. See [Members and invitations](/administration/user-access) and [User roles & permissions](/roles-permissions).

## Ask the agent

Click **New chat** at the top of the sidebar and ask a question in plain words, such as "Which SKUs will stock out in the next 30 days?" See [Agent chat](/agent/chat).
