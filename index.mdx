# Welcome to Tether

Tether is a supply chain planning platform for brands. It brings your products, stock, demand and orders into one place, plans what to buy, make and move, and turns the plan into orders. You can work in the app or ask Tether's agent in plain words.

## What's the difference between Products and Catalog?

**Where:** the **Products** or **Catalog** section of the left sidebar.

They are two versions of Tether's product catalog. Your sidebar shows one of them:

- **Products** (SKUs, SKU Groups, BOMs, Attributes) is the current catalog, used by most organizations. It feeds supply planning directly. Docs: [Products](/principal-catalog/overview).
- **Catalog** (Collections, SKUs) is the older catalog, still used by some older organizations. Its **Collections** and **Dimensions** correspond to SKU groups and attributes in Products. Docs: [SKUs (Catalog)](/product-catalog/skus/overview).

The same split applies to places: organizations on **Locations** manage warehouses, suppliers and channels in **Supply Chain** → **Locations**; older organizations use **Inventory** → **Warehouses** and **Suppliers**, and **Sales** → **Sales Channels**.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **SKU** | One sellable or stockable item, such as a T-shirt in one size and color |
| **SKU group** | A family of related SKUs, such as a style (called a Collection in the older Catalog) |
| **BOM** | Bill of materials: the components that make a SKU |
| **Location** | Any place in your network: warehouse, supplier, factory or sales channel |
| **Policy** | A planning rule on a location or SKU, such as safety stock or MOQ |
| **Channel** | Where you sell (Shopify, Amazon, wholesale) |
| **Transaction** | An order that moves stock: purchase, transfer or production |
| **Sales order** | A customer order, under **Sales** → **Orders** |
| **Shipment** | Goods in transit for one or more orders |
| **Lot** | A batch of stock with its own lot code and dates |
| **Supply Plan** | The plan of orders you've committed to (formerly "Execution plan") |

Your organization may use its own words for some of these; see [Terminology](/administration/terminology).

## What roles are there in Tether?

**Where:** **Settings** → **Access** → **Members**, in the **Role** column.

| Role | For |
|------|-----|
| **Admin** | People who run Tether for your team: planning, inventory, orders, settings, inviting people |
| **Sales Rep** | Sales teams: sales history, forecasts and channels, optionally limited to certain channels |
| **Receiving** | Warehouse staff who record what arrives |
| **Tether Admin** | Tether's support staff |

See [User roles & permissions](/roles-permissions). New here? Start with the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
