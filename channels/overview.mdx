# Sales Channels

A sales channel is where you sell: a Shopify store, a marketplace, a wholesale account. Tether uses channels to group sales orders, forecast demand by channel and report performance.

## Where is the Channels page?

Where: **Sales** → **Sales Channels** (older setup) or **Supply Chain** → **Locations** (newer setup).

<Note>
  **If your sidebar has no Sales Channels entry but has Supply Chain → Locations, your channels live there.** On that setup each channel is a location with the **Sales** capability; filter **Locations** by **Sales** to list them. Old links to `/channels` open that list, and a channel's link opens its location. See [Locations](/supply-chain/locations).
</Note>

On the older setup, **Sales** → **Sales Channels** opens the channels list. It needs channel access (the Sales Rep role or higher).

## How do I add a sales channel?

Where: **Supply Chain** → **Locations** → **+ Location** (tick **Sales**). There is no Add Channel button.

Channels arrive from your supply network:

- **Newer setup (Locations):** **Supply Chain** → **Locations** → **+ Location**, give it a name and tick the **Sales** capability. Then set its **Channel type** and **Currency** on the location's **Overview** → **Channel configuration**.
- **Older setup:** the Sales Channels list shows the channels your supply network already has; when it has none it says "Sales channels arrive from the supply network". Ask Tether to add a channel if you need one.

## Why is my Shopify store not listed as a channel?

Where: **Settings** → **CATALOG** → **Channel Bindings**.

Sales from a feed land on a channel only when the feed's store or marketplace code resolves to one. Tether resolves a binding first and the channel name second. If nothing matches:

1. Make sure the channel exists (see above).
2. Open **Settings** → **CATALOG** → **Channel Bindings**. **Unresolved values** counts the codes a feed sent that matched no channel.
3. Under **Bindings**, bind the store's **External code** (and **Source**) to the right channel. Several codes can point at the same channel. A bound channel can be renamed without breaking its feed.

## The Sales Channels list (older setup)

Where: **Sales** → **Sales Channels**.

- Cards at the top: **Total Channels** and **Active Channels**.
- The table: **Name** (links to the channel's page), **Currency**, and one column per active [channel hierarchy](/channels/hierarchy) field, where you pick each channel's value.
- Header buttons: **Manage Hierarchy** (opens [Channel hierarchy](/channels/hierarchy)) and **Create Manual Order** (opens a new manual sales order).

## Channel detail page (older setup)

Where: **Sales** → **Sales Channels** → a channel name.

The header shows the channel name and an **Inactive** badge if it is inactive, with a **Create Manual Order** button that starts a manual order for this channel. Below are order counts (such as **Total Orders**) and the channel's sales orders. See [Managing channels](/channels/managing).

## Channels and forecasting

Each SKU and channel pair can carry its own forecast, so channels with different demand patterns are planned separately. See [Demand forecasting](/demand-forecasting/dashboard).
