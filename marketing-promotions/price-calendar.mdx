# Price Calendar

The Price Calendar is a preview for planning dated price changes over your real prices: windows of **% off** or a **Sale price**, draft promotions with a discount and expected lift, and the resolved rate each month that they produce.

<Warning>
  **The Price Calendar is a draft preview. Nothing on it is saved.** Your prices and catalog are real, but every window and promotion you add lives in this browser tab only. No promotion is created or changed, and refreshing the page returns it to the starting draft. **Export CSV** is the only way to keep what you build.
</Warning>

## Where is the Price Calendar?

Where: **Marketing & Promotions** → **Price Calendar** (`/marketing-promotions/price-calendar`).

The **Marketing & Promotions** group is shown to organizations that have it turned on, to users with promotions access. If you don't see it, ask Tether.

## How do I record a 20% price cut for next month?

Where: **Marketing & Promotions** → **Price Calendar** → **Windows without a promotion** → **Add a window**.

To try it out on the calendar:

1. Pick the **Season** (**Fall** or **Spring**, and the year) that holds next month, and the channel.
2. Under **Windows without a promotion**, click **Add a window**.
3. Name it, set the start and end dates to next month, choose **% off**, and enter 20.
4. Choose what it **Applies to**: **All items**, a group at the **Group by** level, or one item.
5. Check the **resolved rate by month** grid, then click **Export CSV** to keep it.

This does not change the forecast or your stored prices. For a price cut you want reflected on the forecast, create it as a promotion under [Promotions](/marketing-promotions/promotions) (**Create Promotion**); the Demand Dashboard shows promotions' lift with **Show promo overlay**.

## Where do I see planned price changes?

Where: **Marketing & Promotions** → **Price Calendar** → the resolved-rate grid.

The grid shows, for each row at the **Group by** level, the rate each month resolves to: **Base** where nothing applies, otherwise the rate the windows and promotions covering it resolve to. A cell marked "From a promotion" comes from a draft promotion; a flag means the deepest rate and the narrowest scope disagree on that cell.

Stored promotions are listed under [Promotions](/marketing-promotions/promotions). They carry lift, not price, so they don't appear as price changes here.

## What else is on the page?

Where: **Marketing & Promotions** → **Price Calendar**.

- **Promotions** panel: add a draft promotion (**Add a draft promotion**) with a name, dates, **Discount** or **Sale price**, and **Expected lift**.
- **Projection preview**: pick a row and season dollars to see **Units**, **Dollars**, **AUR** and **Shape %** by month.
- **Reset draft** puts the page back to its starting draft.
