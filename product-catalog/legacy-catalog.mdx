# Legacy catalog (Collections)

Collections are now **SKU groups**. Tether's catalog moved to the **Products** section of the sidebar, where collections became primary SKU groups, dimensions became attributes, and the SKU page gained Purchasing, Sales and Cost & price tabs. Every organization is moving to Products.

## Which catalog am I on?

Where: look at your sidebar.

- **Products → SKUs, SKU Groups, BOMs, Attributes:** you're on the current catalog. Use [Products](/principal-catalog/overview). Your collections are your [primary SKU groups](/principal-catalog/sku-groups). Old links to `/collections` and `/skus` open the new pages.
- **Catalog → Collections, SKUs:** you're on the legacy catalog, described below. It appears only for organizations that haven't moved yet. To move to Products, ask Tether.

## What's where in the legacy catalog

| You want to… | Legacy catalog | Products (current) |
|--------------|----------------|--------------------|
| See all SKUs | **Catalog → SKUs** | **Products → SKUs** |
| Group SKUs into a product | **Catalog → Collections** | **Products → SKU Groups** (primary group) |
| Generate size/color variants | The collection's **SKUs** tab (SKU generator) | **Bulk Create SKUs** on a primary SKU group |
| Define sizes and colors | The collection's **Dimensions** tab | **Products → Attributes** |
| Import SKUs | **Import SKUs** page (`/skus/import`) | **Import CSV** on the SKU list |
| Categorize products | **Settings → Collection Hierarchy** | **Settings → SKU Group Hierarchy** |
| Set a SKU's price | The SKU's **Pricing** tab | The SKU's **Sales** tab; see [Cost & price](/principal-catalog/cost-and-price) |

## Legacy SKU page tabs

A SKU opened from **Catalog → SKUs** has these tabs: **Overview**, **Bill of Materials**, **Inventory**, **Pricing**, **Network**, and **Suppliers** (only when the SKU belongs to a collection).

## Legacy collection page tabs

A collection opened from **Catalog → Collections** has these tabs: **Overview**, **Dimensions**, **SKUs**, **Hierarchy**, **Inventory**, **Suppliers**, **Pricing**, **BoM** and **Settings**.

To add SKUs to a collection, open the collection's **SKUs** tab.
